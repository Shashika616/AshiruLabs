# Image credits

Every photograph on this site is **CC0** (public-domain dedication): free for commercial use, no
attribution required. The provenance is recorded here anyway, so the site can show where each
picture came from.

All were sourced from Wikimedia Commons, accepting only files whose machine-readable licence
field reads CC0 or Public domain. Each was then cropped, converted to greyscale, softened and
re-encoded locally. The site's palette is applied to them in the 3D scene's shader.

## Rules for replacing them

- **CC0 or public domain only.** Not CC BY, not "free to use" stock licences, not anything
  with conditions.
- **No identifiable people.** CC0 waives the photographer's copyright, not the personality
  rights of whoever is pictured.
- **No logos, brand marks or branded hardware** (keyboards with an OS key, laptops with a maker's
  badge, and so on).
- **No famous landmarks.** Some, like the Eiffel Tower's night lighting, carry their own copyright.
