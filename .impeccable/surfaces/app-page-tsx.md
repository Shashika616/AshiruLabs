# Surface: AshiruLabs home (app/page.tsx)

Mode: Persuade. Audience: founders / product leads choosing a team to build their software; secondary: TieHere-curious visitors, hires.
Action: start a project conversation (contact). Secondary: visit tiehere.com.
Proof: TieHere shipped (real product facts only). No invented clients, metrics, testimonials.
Constraints: Next.js, Three.js hero, heavily optimized, mobile responsive, reduced-motion fallback, content readable without WebGL.
Seed key: 37e5591d (assigned index 4: survey-station world, inside the user-pinned dark glass-stair aesthetic).

## Direction contract

THESIS: The climb is the site. One continuous glass staircase spans the whole page; a lone figure climbs it as you scroll and descends when you scroll back. Each landing is a surveyed station where a chapter of content stands. Refuses the category default: a hero image followed by stacked card grids.

OWN-WORLD: Near-black ground, cold white light, survey hairline grid, crosshair corner brackets on every control and frame, station coordinates and elevation in tabular numerals. One faint TieHere blue/green only where TieHere is on stage. Glass treads with bright edge light and specular stars.

STORY: Every product decision is a measured step → we ship our own (TieHere, the proof) → we build yours the same way (services, process) → the summit is a conversation.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Headline left, the staircase rising right with the figure on the first treads, bracketed CTAs, a live elevation readout at the edge. Mobile: stairs fill behind the copy, readout at the top.

SIGNATURE: Scroll-scrubbed ascent: camera tracks the figure step by step with damped (spring) follow; the elevation gauge counts metres with trend, station markers tick as landings pass.

RISK: 3D cost on low-end phones; mitigated by DPR caps, a lazy chunk, an adaptive quality tier, and a static fallback.

Raises: from the night-flight six-pack (declined: loses audience identification), damped instrument readout with trend instead of a bare progress bar. Ice-press, flyer wall, alphabet storm, cityscape, minihompy: declined (break the pinned monochrome glass world); cityscape kept its rain-sheen reflections idea as the floor's faint reflection.
